How I work

From requirements to handoff

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Gather requirements with stakeholders and agree on what matters first.

  2. 02

    Flows & wireframes

    Map user flows and structure before any pixels get polished.

  3. 03

    Hi-fi & prototype

    Responsive high-fidelity screens and prototypes in Figma, aligned with the design system.

  4. 04

    Client review

    Present progress and decisions directly to the client, then iterate.

  5. 05

    Handoff & sprints

    Hand off to developers and stay in sprint planning until it ships.
